# Harness 适配器

Harness 适配器将 `HarnessAgent` 连接到特定的智能体运行时。它们是
AI SDK 模型提供商的 Harness 等价物：每个适配器封装一个运行时，
并将其会话、流事件、工具、用量、生命周期状态和配置规范化为 Harness 契约。

## AI SDK Harness 适配器

AI SDK 包含以下 Harness 适配器：

- [Claude Code](/providers/ai-sdk-harnesses/claude-code) (`@ai-sdk/harness-claude-code`)
- [Cline](/providers/ai-sdk-harnesses/cline) (`@ai-sdk/harness-cline`)
- [Codex](/providers/ai-sdk-harnesses/codex) (`@ai-sdk/harness-codex`)
- [Deep Agents](/providers/ai-sdk-harnesses/deepagents) (`@ai-sdk/harness-deepagents`)
- [Grok Build](/providers/ai-sdk-harnesses/grok-build) (`@ai-sdk/harness-grok-build`)
- [OpenCode](/providers/ai-sdk-harnesses/opencode) (`@ai-sdk/harness-opencode`)
- [Pi](/providers/ai-sdk-harnesses/pi) (`@ai-sdk/harness-pi`)

### 即将推出

- Amp (`@ai-sdk/harness-amp`)
- Goose (`@ai-sdk/harness-goose`)
- Mastra (`@ai-sdk/harness-mastra`)

| 适配器                                                  | 运行时位置       | 自定义工具 | 自定义技能 | 结构化输出 | 内置工具审批 | 内置工具过滤                |
| ------------------------------------------------------ | ---------------- | ------------ | ------------- | ----------------- | ---------------------- | ---------------------------- |
| [Claude Code](/providers/ai-sdk-harnesses/claude-code) | 沙箱桥接         | <Check />    | <Check />     | <Check />         | <Check />              | <Check />                    |
| [Cline](/providers/ai-sdk-harnesses/cline)             | 宿主进程         | <Check />    | <Check />     | <Check />         | <Check />              | <Check />                    |
| [Codex](/providers/ai-sdk-harnesses/codex)             | 沙箱桥接         | <Check />    | <Check />     | <Check />         | <Cross />              | <Cross />                    |
| [Deep Agents](/providers/ai-sdk-harnesses/deepagents)  | 沙箱桥接         | <Check />    | <Check />     | <Check />         | <Check />              | <Check /> 通过自动拒绝       |
| [Grok Build](/providers/ai-sdk-harnesses/grok-build)   | 通过 ACP 的沙箱 | <Check />    | <Check />     | <Check />         | <Check />              | <Cross />                    |
| [OpenCode](/providers/ai-sdk-harnesses/opencode)       | 沙箱桥接         | <Check />    | <Check />     | <Check />         | <Check />              | <Check /> 通过自动拒绝       |
| [Pi](/providers/ai-sdk-harnesses/pi)                   | 宿主进程         | <Check />    | <Check />     | <Cross />         | <Check />              | <Check />                    |

通用 ACP 配置文件不支持结构化输出，除非它们配置了
实现特定的提示词元数据映射；Grok Build 包含所需的
映射，而 Codex ACP 未提供此类映射。
